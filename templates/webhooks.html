Webhooks

Fired when a token in {{.Env.Slug}} is issued, redeemed, spent or revoked. The body is signed with X-Webhook-Signature: sha256=HMAC(secret, body); verify it against the raw bytes before parsing. Failed deliveries are retried immediately, then after 30s, 2m and 10m.

The token itself is never sent. A delivery carries the id, the prefix and the metadata — enough to correlate, useless to an interceptor.
